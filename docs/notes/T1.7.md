## Public API (as implemented)

Levels in brackets.

- **`text(e, ctx)` [3]**: the canonical answer text (DESIGN 12.1), or `None` when it would be
  longer than `MAXTEXT = 4000` characters (the record is then `Done`). `ctx` is the evaluation
  context list `[deg, mainvar, approx, inert]` (`P.X_DEG`, `P.X_MV`, ...), or `None` (radian,
  no command variable). The steps:
  1. **Display pass**: one bottom-up walk (`A.postorder`) through the constructors:
     - in degree mode, each `ASIN/ACOS/ATAN(u)` becomes `(pi/180)*CALL(SYM('asin'), u)`;
     - **sum_content** (fmt 1.3 step 3): in every product, the rational content of each factor
       `S^1` (`S` a sum whose coefficients are all real rationals) moves to the product's
       coefficient, and the product is rebuilt with `mul_`. So `y*(2x+2)` prints `2*y*(x+1)`,
       `e^(2x)*(2sin(3x)/13-3cos(3x)/13)` prints `e^(2*x)*(2*sin(3*x)-3*cos(3*x))/13`,
       `2(x/2+1/2)` prints `x+1`, and `(x+2)(2x+4)` prints `2*(x+2)^2`.
  2. The explicit-stack driver of 12.2 over `items`. A top-level list of relations (or of
     lists of relations) prints bare: `x=1,x=2`, `[x=1,y=2],[x=3,y=4]`.
  3. **Long polynomial over an integer** (fmt 1.4): when the text is longer than
     `LONGPOLY = 70` characters and the (display) root is `MUL(p/q, S, 1)` with `q > 1`, `S` a
     sum without `O()`, the root is replaced by its expansion `add_([S], p/q)` and printed
     again term by term. If that second text is over `MAXTEXT`, the first text is kept.
  4. `K.release(m0)`: every node built by `text` is display-only and is released before the
     return. `text` leaves `K.NN` unchanged.
- **`items(n, need, c)` [4]**: one node's display sequence (literal strings and `(child,
  need)` pairs), in parentheses when its own precedence is below `need`. `c = (deg, mv)`.
  `None` for an integer over the `str()` guard.
- **`record(val, job)` [2]**: `job = (kind, input text, ctx, flags)` as `R.go` passes it;
  `ctx` and `flags` may be missing (`ctx` then comes from `F_DEG`). Returns `(kind, text,
  approx, flags)`:
  - `val is None` gives `('msg', 'undefined', None, 0)`;
  - with `F_SOLN`: an empty list or `FALSE` is `no solution`; `TRUE` is `every x is a
    solution`, with the main variable's name;
  - anywhere: `DNE(l, r)` is `does not exist: l from the left, r from the right` (each side cut
    to 40 characters plus `...`), or `no limit (it oscillates)` when a side is `UNDEF`;
    `DIVG(w)` is one of the three §10.5 `diverges (...)` messages;
  - a text over `MAXTEXT` is `('msg', 'Done', None, 0)`;
  - an `eval` job gets the decimal line (`dline`), and `F_APPROX` when the answer holds a FLT
    although `ctx[X_APPROX]` is 0 (a numeric fallback or `approx()`); the input flags are
    passed through.
- **`dline(e)` [3]**: the 12.6 decimal line or `None`; any exception gives `None`. Exact
  rationals (also Gaussian) use `dec10` directly; everything else `A.nprog`/`A.nrun` and
  `fdec10`, complex as `a+bi` (imaginary rounding noise under 1e-13 of the larger part is
  dropped). Never for integers, Gaussian integers, floats, symbols, lists, relations, calls,
  `O()`, sentinels, `oo`, `undef`, `true`, `false`, or a size of 64 or more.
- **Display-order helpers**: `xpow(t, mv)` [7] (floor(60 x the exponent of mv)),
  `tkey(t)` [7], `pkey(b)` [7] (FlowCE `poly_key`), `scont(b)` [7] (sum content `(g, l)` or
  `None`), `sorder(n, mv)` [6] (the display order of a sum's terms), `pdisp(n, k, c)` [5]
  (the display of a product or of the sum term `k*n`), `pickmv(e)` [7], `mainv(e, ctx)` [6],
  `bare(e)` [7].
- **Number text**: `istr`, `imstr`, `rstr`, `fstr` (FLT: 15 digits through `dec10`, with `.0`
  added when there is no point and no `E`, so it re-lexes as a float: `2.0`, `1E10`, `0.5`),
  `cfmt` (decimal line `a+bi`).
- **Constants**: `MAXTEXT = 4000`, `LONGPOLY = 70`, `DECBITS`, record kinds `R_ANS = 'ans'`,
  `R_MSG = 'msg'`, flags `F_PLUSC 1`, `F_PURGEHINT 2`, `F_APPROX 4`, `F_DEG 8`, `F_SOLN 16`
  (the same bits as `run.py`; `F_SOLN == P.F_SOLN`), the messages `M_*`, the output
  precedences `Q_*` (the `rpn` levels of §7.7).

## Display rules as implemented (12.3, 12.4)

- **Sums**: descending `x_power`, the constant after the terms of power 0 (so `x+1+1/x`); ties
  by `tkey` = (total symbol degree descending, kind: symbol < product < function, trig in the
  order sin cos sec csc tan cot), then `H`, then the id. Two terms: the positive one first,
  then the real part first. A series (a sum with an `O()` term) is ascending with `O()` last,
  and keeps that order with two terms too (`-x+O(x^3)`). A complex constant prints as its real
  and imaginary parts.
- **Main variable**: `ctx[X_MV]`, else `x`, `t`, `theta`, else the first name; a call's name
  (`f` of `f(x)`, the degree-mode `asin` atom) is not a variable. A sum free of the main
  variable orders by its own.
- **Products**: numbers (with numeric radicals), `pi`, variables, `e^u` and `2^x`, then
  polynomials by `poly_key`, trig by argument size and name, the rest by `H`. Coefficient
  `p*m/q`; a negative exponent goes below the bar; `^(1/2)` is `sqrt`; the minus in front
  (`-3/(16*(x-1))`); `LN10^-m` with LN factors of exponents summing to m prints `log`.
  A sum factor under an integer exponent whose displayed first term is negative is flipped:
  `(2-x^2)*sin(x)` prints `-(x^2-2)*sin(x)`, `2x*cos(x)-(2-x^2)*sin(x)` prints
  `2*x*cos(x)+(x^2-2)*sin(x)`.
- **Names**: `asin acos atan`; `true false`; `x>3` for `3<x` (LT/LE swap when the main variable
  is only on the right; EQ and NE are never swapped, so the round trip holds); `HOLD` pairs
  as built, `2^3*3^2*5`; `O(u)`; calls `f(a,b)`; lists `[a,b]`; `n!`, `(n+1)!`; a relation
  right after `!` gets a space: `n! =1` (`n!=1` would lex as `n != 1`).
- **Degree mode**: trig arguments print as `v*180/pi`, term by term through the constructors
  (`sin(x+30)`), inverse trig through the print-only `CALL` atom (`asin(x)+30`).

## Deviations from DESIGN.md (and why)

1. **Round-trip exceptions (12.5 lists only HOLD).** 12.3 and 8.8 make `sum_content` and the
   long-polynomial rule the printer's job, but the constructors keep `2(x+1)` and `(x+1)/2`
   (§5.3), so the printed text of `y*(2x+2)` re-parses to `MUL(2, y, x+1)`, not to `MUL(1, y,
   2x+2)`. Both are canonical nodes of the same value. The round trip is therefore defined
   against a precise normal form, computed independently in `tests/test_roundtrip.py`:
   - HOLD re-parses to its value (as designed);
   - a one-element top-level list of one relation prints as the relation (FlowCE's `x=2`)
     and re-parses to it;
   - `cnorm(e)`: the content of every factor `S^1` moved to its product's coefficient,
     bottom-up through the constructors;
   - a long `(sum)/q` re-parses to its expansion.

   Radian mode: `reparse(text(e)) == expected(e)` exactly. Degree mode: the content is that
   of the degree display (`y*(asin(x)+90)` has none, although its radian node does), so the
   test compares `cnorm` of both sides. Every mode: the text is a fixed point,
   `text(reparse(text(e))) == text(e)` (except HOLD). Text-only session state stays safe: the
   re-parsed value is equal and prints the same.
2. **The content is computed bottom-up**, on the display tree (children first, as FlowCE's
   `positive_first`), so `z*(y*(2x+2)+2w)` prints `2*z*(w+y*(x+1))` and the text is a fixed
   point. Only factors with exponent 1 are touched (FlowCE: `(2x+2)^2` and `1/(2x+2)` stay).
   The FlowCE "make it negative when every term is" part is covered by the flip rule.
3. **The long-polynomial rule** applies whenever the coefficient after `sum_content` has a
   denominator, including `2*(...)/91` (FlowCE checks that giac's numerator is a sum; ours is
   `2*(sum)` after the content moved out). Top level only, as FlowCE.
4. **Sum tie-break `tkey`** before `H` (12.3 says `H`, then the id): `sin(x)^2+cos(x)^2`,
   `b^2+b`, a symbol before a product, instead of hash order.
5. **The flip** looks at the first displayed term of the sum (after the two-term swap), for
   any sum factor under an integer exponent, also outside sums (FlowCE: the leading power for a
   polynomial, else a majority of negative terms, and only for negated terms of a sum).
6. **Not done:** FlowCE's "negated product holding a 2-term difference" flip
   (`-(cos(x)-sin(x))*u` as `(sin(x)-cos(x))*u`, `answer.cc:472-484`); 12.3 does not list
   it. Which of the two forms prints depends on the constructors' sign normalization (hash
   order), deterministically.
7. **A lone sum is never content-factored**: `cos(5pi/12)` prints `sqrt(6)/4-sqrt(2)/4`
   (T1.1's notes expected `(sqrt(6)-sqrt(2))/4` from the printer; FlowCE's rule is for factors
   of products, and `(sqrt(6)-sqrt(2))/4` would re-parse to a different node). If that form is
   wanted, the special-value table (T1.1) can return `MUL(1/4, sqrt(6)-sqrt(2), 1)`, which is
   canonical and prints that way.
8. **`text` releases its display nodes** (12.2 says the table is reset after the command
   anyway). This bounds the nodes of repeated `text` calls (CVFORM, the two DNE sides, the
   long-polynomial second pass) under `NMAX`.
9. **Sentinels inside a result** (a `DNE` in a list) print `undef`; a `WILD` prints `_wN`
   (rule debugging only).
10. **Golden files `messages.txt` and `decimal.txt`** (20.6) are not in T1.7's file list, so
    they were not created. The decimal-line cases are in `test_print.py` (`DEC`, `NODEC`, the
    node cases). Printer messages for `messages.txt`: `Done`, `undefined`, `no solution`,
    `every %s is a solution`, `does not exist: %s from the left, %s from the right`,
    `no limit (it oscillates)`, `diverges (the improper integral does not converge)`,
    `diverges (the integrand is unbounded on the interval)`, `diverges (the terms do not go
    to 0)`.

## Tests

- `tests/test_print.py`: 102 tests. The 256-case golden file (`print_t1.txt`: fmt 1.1-1.4
  orders, sum content, flips, quotients, powers, roots, `e`, `log`, functions, relations,
  lists, solutions, series, numbers, floats, degree mode); the keys (`xpow`, `pkey`, `scont`,
  `sorder`, the main variable); names, HOLD, relations, factorials, calls, floats, Gaussian
  numbers, degree mode; `MAXTEXT` boundary and the 30,000-digit `Done` in under 0.5 s; the
  record shapes, every sentinel message, flags; 30 decimal lines and 20 cases without one;
  nesting invariance of the measured depth (8 kinds x 1/8/32); strict depth; random trees;
  the invariant scan of the display nodes before `text` releases them; `text` leaves `K.NN`
  unchanged.
- `tests/test_roundtrip.py`: 63 tests (23 not slow). Every golden case in its own mode and in
  the other mode; random trees (with floats, with polynomial products and their powers, with
  answer shapes: relations, lists, calls, `O()`, solutions, factorials); a sum-content fuzz
  (products of contentful sums nested in sums, powers, functions, relations, lists, both
  modes); named content, long-polynomial, solution, series, Gaussian, HOLD, degree-mode and
  factorial cases.
- lint and FRAMELINT clean; `--strict-depth` passes. Longest chain through the printer:
  `1 run.go > 2 printer.record > 3 printer.text > 4 printer.items > 5 node.mul_ > 6 node.neg_ >
  7 node.mk` (7).

## T1.11 completion follow-up (2026-10-11)

`items` now prints negative real exponents inside a `HOLD` as denominator factors, retaining
prime powers: `factor(360/7)` gives `2^3*3^2*5/7`, and `factor(1/36)` gives `1/(2^2*3^2)`.
The implicit numerator is `1` (or `-1`) when all prime powers are below the bar. Multiple
denominator factors are parenthesized. Six factor-command regression cases check exact value,
printed text and re-parsing. Display-only positive exponent nodes are released by `text` as usual.
