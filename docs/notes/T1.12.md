## Current entry points

- `solve.g_solve(args, ctx)` [2]: registry entry for an equation or a system, with an optional
  symbol or variable list. A scalar result is a list of equalities, an empty list, or `TRUE`.
- `solve.g_roots(f, x, md)` [2]: checked, sorted roots as a tuple; `TRUE` for an identity;
  `None` on a soft failure. `md` selects degree mode.
- `solve.g_eq(f, x, dep, md)` [2]: candidate search after normalization/factoring;
  `g_fin` and `g_ok` finish and verify candidates. Limits: `MAXDEP = 8`, `MAXROOTS = 24`.
- `solveb.g_poly`, `g_fac`, `g_inv` [2]: polynomial formulas, kernel inverses, radical
  elimination and trig cases. `INV` contains inverse templates with guards.
- `solvec.g_sys`, `g_elim` [2]: system orchestration and substitution elimination;
  `lsys` [3] uses rational Gauss-Jordan; `nsolve` [3] samples and refines numerical roots,
  checking residuals to reject poles. `MAXVARS = 8`, `NS = 400`.
